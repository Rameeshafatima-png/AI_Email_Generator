## Overview

**Dispatch** is a modern AI-powered email generator that transforms a simple idea into a polished, well-structured email.

Instead of spending time deciding how to start an email, what tone to use, or how to structure the message, users can simply provide the recipient, describe what they need to communicate, choose a tone, and let Gemini generate the email.

The application combines a lightweight **FastAPI backend**, **Google Gemini AI**, and a custom **responsive frontend** designed around a digital writing desk.
